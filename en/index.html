What I do

Available for hire

I work with AI: I get to grips with the task, build a solution, make sure it works and see it through.

  1. Telegram bots and mini apps

    A catalogue, orders, bookings or enquiries — right inside Telegram, with no separate website or app. Example: the Si Phone store

  2. AI assistants

    A bot that answers customer questions from your own knowledge base: products, prices, delivery, FAQs.

  3. Websites and landing pages

    A company website, landing page or portfolio — custom design, lively animation and a proper mobile version.

  4. Data collection and processing

    Information from websites and open sources, organised into spreadsheets and turned into reports. Example: tender automation

  5. Automating routine work

    Services, spreadsheets and documents connected so that repetitive work gets done on its own.

  6. Prototyping ideas

    A quick working version to test an idea before you invest serious money in it.

Describe your task in a couple of sentences — I’ll tell you whether it’s doable and how I’d approach it.
