Telegram Mini App

Si Phone

An Apple tech store that opens right inside Telegram: catalogue, product configurator, cart and checkout, plus an admin panel for the owner to manage products, prices and photos. I built it for a friend’s business: the store has launched and is taking orders.

How it works

  • TelegramThe customer opens the store from the bot — no installation, no sign-up. Light and dark themes that follow Telegram, the native Back and Checkout buttons, haptic feedback.
  • BotAn “Open store” button, answers to common questions, customer reviews and a way to reach a manager.
  • Mini appA React interface designed for phones: search, catalogue, configurator, comparison, cart, checkout. Customer reviews from the store’s channel and a gift wheel with discounts on the next order.
  • Catalogue and pricesProducts and prices live in a Supabase database, and rouble prices are recalculated from the Central Bank exchange rate automatically. The storefront is served through a cache on Cloudflare: a load test of up to 1,000 simultaneous requests ran without errors.
  • OrderingPrices and totals are calculated by the server from the database, not by the cart on the phone. Each order goes straight to the managers as a Telegram message.
  • Admin panel in TelegramSign-in with a Telegram account from the admin list. Products and prices, supplier price lists as text or Excel, descriptions and specs, photos prepared automatically, a change history.

Security and compliance

  • The store is built with Russia’s personal data law (152-FZ) in mind: it has a privacy policy and a separate consent form, and you can’t place an order without giving consent.
  • The store doesn’t keep customers’ personal data: orders are stored anonymised, and only the managers see names, phone numbers and addresses, in Telegram.
  • Customers are identified through Telegram, and the server verifies it: prices, order totals and admin rights can’t be faked from the phone.
  • The database is locked down, and the store was checked for vulnerabilities before launch.

Results

  • A working store in Telegram: customers choose their tech and place an order, managers get it in their chat, and the owner updates products and prices in the admin panel.
  • I started with a prototype on demo data and took it all the way to launch: a new catalogue, a design that feels native to Telegram, an admin panel, security and lawful handling of personal data.
  • I don’t quote sales figures: that’s my friend’s business data.
